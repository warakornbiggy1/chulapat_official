About CHULAPAT

More than a sports color squad — a troupe that brings together people of every talent to put on a show of friendship.

The Beginning

From Friends to a Legend

CHULAPAT began in 2019 with friends who believed color sports aren't just about winning — they're a stage where everyone gets to shine. We chose a dark circus theme because the big top stands for diversity: every performer has a part, and together they become something spectacular.

Orange & black is our heart — orange for energy and joy, black for grit and resolve.
